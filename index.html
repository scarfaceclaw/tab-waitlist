Who's behind it

Tab is designed and built by a builder, not a software company. I've been in the building trade for over 30 years, and more than 90% of the trades on my sites are subcontractors. Getting invoicing sorted correctly and easily has always been a problem for them: CIS worked out by hand, apps that cap you then put the price up, paperwork left in the van until the end of the month.

Tab comes from years of hearing those complaints and watching good tradespeople go round in circles with the admin, or bury their heads in the sand. The point is to make it simple enough that you can get on with what you do best.
